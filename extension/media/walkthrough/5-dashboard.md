## See everything

The **Steerhouse** icon in the activity bar shows every session, its sub-agents and every change.

- Click a change for the real before/after diff, then click the gutter to comment. The comment goes to the agent.
- **Dashboard → Graph** shows prompts, sub-agents and the files they touched. Prompts from different sessions that changed the same file are connected.
- Drag images onto a session to send them to that agent.
